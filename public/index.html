Microphone check

Before recording, a short check of your microphone and room, so every take meets a minimum quality. Use the microphone and the place you will record with.

  1. Stay quiet for three seconds while the room noise is measured.
  2. Then read this sentence aloud and press Stop: Elen síla lúmenn' omentielvo.

    How to read
    • Use a quiet room and a headset or USB microphone, always the same one, a hand's width from your mouth. Not the laptop's built-in microphone.
    • Read calmly and clearly, as you would speak it. Leave a short silence before and after each line.
    • Pronounce it as Third Age Quenya: long vowels long, double consonants long, r trilled. Listen plays a computer reading of the line, when there is one, if you are unsure.
    • Word lists (Valtan, loica, oiavë.) are read with a small pause at each comma.
    • Keys: Space to record and stop, P to play back, L to listen, arrows for the previous and next line.
    • Every take is checked as soon as you stop. A take that is clipped, almost silent or empty is not kept: record it again. Other problems (noise, a cut-off ending, a long pause) are shown and the take is kept, so you can decide whether to record it again. Recording a line again replaces its take.
