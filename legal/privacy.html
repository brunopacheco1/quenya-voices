Quenya Voices is a website where invited volunteers record themselves reading Quenya, to build an open collection of recordings and an open text-to-speech voice for Quenya. This notice explains what personal data the site handles, why, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

When recordings are used for training

Recordings are used to train a speech model only if you gave your consent to it (when you accepted the licence) and a reviewer accepted them, and only once the accepted recordings of all volunteers together reach {{minimum_training_minutes}} minutes of speech. The first training starts no earlier than {{training_wait_days}} days after that minimum is reached, so everyone has time to check their data or ask for erasure. Later trainings start whenever needed. The current totals are public on the progress page.

We do not use your voice to identify you, we do not profile you, and we make no automated decisions that affect you. The automatic checks on each take only ask you to record it again when it is clipped, silent or empty. We collect no special categories of data and no data about anyone else.

Who receives your data

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the site, the sign-in (Cloudflare Access) and the storage (R2), as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum. Recordings are stored in Cloudflare's EU jurisdiction. Cloudflare is a US company; transfers it may make outside the EU are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Reviewers chosen by the project hear your submitted recordings and see the name you want to be credited by, but not your e-mail address.
  • The public: the trained models, and the name you want to be credited by. Your recordings themselves only if you chose to publish them under CC BY 4.0; then anyone may copy and use them under that licence. Note that a model trained on your voice can produce speech that sounds like you.

Your rights

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • see and download all your data at any time: My data on the site gives you all of it, and you can download your recordings (access and portability, art. 15 and 20);
  • correct the name you are credited by: My data (rectification, art. 16);
  • withdraw your consent and have your data erased, at any time: My data → Delete my account, or by writing to us (art. 7(3) and 17). We then delete your data from our storage and our backups and leave it out of every future training and release. Erasure cannot reach what was already done with your consent before: models already trained on your recordings, and, if you chose to publish your recordings under CC BY, copies that others made of them;
  • restrict or object to processing, by writing to us (art. 18 and 21).

For anything else, write to {{contact_email}}. We answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, in Luxembourg the {{supervisory_authority}}, or the one where you live or work.

Age

The site is only for people aged 18 or over.

Cookies

The site uses only the cookies needed to sign you in, and no tracking. See the Cookie Policy.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date above changes, and we tell you on the site before you record again. For changes that need new consent, we ask for it.
