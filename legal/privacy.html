Quenya Voices is a website where invited volunteers record themselves reading Quenya, to build an open text-to-speech voice for Quenya. The recordings themselves are never published. This notice explains what personal data the site handles, why, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

When recordings are used for training

Recordings are used to train a speech model only if you gave your consent to it (when you accepted the terms) and a reviewer accepted them, and only once the accepted recordings of all volunteers together reach {{minimum_training_minutes}} minutes of speech. The first training starts no earlier than {{training_wait_days}} days after that minimum is reached, so everyone has time to check their data or ask for erasure. Later trainings start whenever needed. The current totals are public on the progress page.

Published models speak with an artificial voice, not yours. Before each release we compare that voice with the voices of the accepted recordings, only to make sure it does not sound like any volunteer; the comparison is done on the training computer and not kept. Published models are licensed so that imitating real people is forbidden, and everything they produce carries a watermark that marks it as AI-generated. A model still learns from every voice it hears, so it may produce speech that sounds somewhat like you.

We do not use your voice to identify you, we do not profile you, and we make no automated decisions that affect you. The automatic checks on each take only ask you to record it again when it is clipped, silent or empty. We collect no special categories of data and no data about anyone else.

Who receives your data

  • Gcore, a Luxembourg company, hosts the site and stores your data in its data centres in the European Union, as our processor under its data processing agreement. Everything is encrypted by the site before it is stored, so Gcore cannot read your recordings or your details.
  • {{mail_provider}} sends the e-mails with your sign-in codes, as our processor. It receives your e-mail address and the code, nothing else. Its data processing agreement allows some of its subprocessors to be outside the European Union (for example in the United States), under the safeguards that agreement sets out.
  • Reviewers chosen by the project hear your submitted recordings and see the name you want to be credited by, but not your e-mail address.
  • Gcore also provides the GPU machine that trains the model. For each training, accepted recordings are copied over HTTPS to a machine created for that training alone, in one of Gcore's data centres in the European Union, and deleted with it, disk included, when the training ends (at the latest after a few hours). Each training copies the recordings again, so erased recordings are never in it.
  • The public: the trained models (with an artificial voice), audio made with them, and the name you want to be credited by. Never your recordings.

How we protect it

Your recordings and details stay in the European Union (only the sign-in e-mail goes through {{mail_provider}}, as above) and are encrypted in transit (the site and every copy for training use HTTPS only) and at rest: the site encrypts every recording and every detail about you (AES-256) before storing it, with a key that only the site and the training have. Copies for training are temporary and deleted with the training machine. The site keeps no visitor logs. Only you, the reviewers (your submitted recordings) and the person running the training (accepted recordings) can reach your recordings.

Your rights

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • see and download all your data at any time: My data on the site gives you all of it, and you can download your recordings (access and portability, art. 15 and 20);
  • correct the name you are credited by: My data (rectification, art. 16);
  • withdraw your consent and have your data erased, at any time: My data → Delete my account, or by writing to us (art. 7(3) and 17). We then delete your data from our storage and our backups and leave it out of every future training and release. Erasure cannot reach what was already done with your consent before: models already trained on your recordings stay as they are;
  • restrict or object to processing, by writing to us (art. 18 and 21).

For anything else, write to {{contact_email}}. We answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, in Luxembourg the {{supervisory_authority}}, or the one where you live or work.

Age

The site is only for people aged 18 or over.

Changes
