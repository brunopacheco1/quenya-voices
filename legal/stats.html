How the recordings are used

Training starts only when the accepted recordings reach {{minimum_training_minutes}} minutes, and the first training waits {{training_wait_days}} days after that, so every volunteer has time to check their data or ask for erasure. Only recordings whose volunteers consented to training are used, and a volunteer who asks for erasure is left out of every training after that.
