4. Review, training and the published voice

Reviewers listen to submitted recordings. They may accept them, or ask you to record some lines again, saying why. We are not obliged to use any recording.

Recordings are used for training only once the accepted recordings of all volunteers reach {{minimum_training_minutes}} minutes, and the first training waits {{training_wait_days}} days after that. The progress page shows where we are.

To protect your voice, published models:

  • speak with an artificial voice, not the voice of any volunteer. Before each release we check that it does not sound like any of the voices it was trained on;
  • are published under a licence that forbids imitating real people without their consent, and any deceptive use;
  • mark everything they produce with an inaudible watermark and a label saying it is AI-generated, as the EU AI Act requires.

A model learns from every voice it hears, so it may still produce speech that sounds somewhat like you, especially if someone uses it with a sample of your voice.

5. Withdrawing

You can download all your data, delete your drafts, and ask for erasure of your account and all your recordings at any time under My data. We then delete them from our storage and backups and leave them out of every future training and release. This does not undo what was done before: models already trained on them stay, and so do copies you published yourself. See the Privacy Notice.
