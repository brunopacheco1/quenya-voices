Reviewers listen to submitted recordings. They may accept them, or ask you to record some lines again, saying why. We are not obliged to publish or use any recording. Accepted recordings may be published as open data (if you chose so), and used to train open speech models that are published too.

Recordings are used for training only once the accepted recordings of all volunteers reach {{minimum_training_minutes}} minutes, and the first training waits {{training_wait_days}} days after that. The progress page shows where we are.

5. Withdrawing

You can download all your data, delete your drafts, and ask for erasure of your account and all your recordings at any time under My data. We then delete them from our storage and backups and leave them out of every future training and release. This does not undo what was done before: models already trained on them stay, and if you chose to publish your recordings under CC BY, copies that others already made stay under that licence.
